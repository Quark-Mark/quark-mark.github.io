Radio drama and multimedia

The Glass Snail Speaks

An original audio adaptation shaped for voices, music, silence and acoustic space.

The radio drama is a separate work. The literary version is presented under Literature.

ACT FOUR
THE GLASS SNAIL SPEAKS

(SOUND: A room with a wooden ship-like floor. Footsteps on wood. Two clocks calling to one another. TICK. TOCK. TICK. TOCK. But differently. Asynchronously.)

THE GLASS SNAIL

(a bodiless voice, reverberant, slow)

The shadows of objects in the drawing room were Renaissance-sharp. Shaded only by natural light. By the whiteness of a day.

(SOUND: The quietest sound. As though the air were standing still.)

THE GLASS SNAIL

Silence curled up beside my head. From my centre one could see the outlines of Pavić’s ghost and Miss Hatchepust. Distorted. Unrecognisable. Abstract outlines of beings dancing their elongated games.

THE GLASS SNAIL

In my dream I heard the words of Pavić’s ghost. I wove them into the dream. I tucked meanings into the folds of two clocks speaking tick-tock. A bile-coloured quarrel. Fragments. Broken words.

PAVIĆ

(far away, as if from a dream)

I know what your name is.

HATCHEPUST

(far away)

How do you know?

(SOUND: Glass ringing for a long time. Then — silence.)

THE GLASS SNAIL

I did not care what content was taken from me or placed inside me, as long as I fulfilled my essence as a vessel. I existed only to contain. Or not to contain. And nothing else.

(SOUND: Winds. Ancient. Desert winds.)

THE GLASS SNAIL

Centuries taught me to have as few longing desires as possible. For desire leads to repetition. Repetition is slavery. Slavery is illusion. Illusion is pain.
